How it works

  • Each upgrade raises a creep's level by one (+1000 hits, +100 store). Taking a power from level k to k+1 needs the creep to already be at the level shown on that pip. A build is shown red when no upgrade order reaches it.
  • Only the operator class exists. Commander and executor were announced but never shipped, so they are not here.
  • Ops are costed per power at a cast interval you can edit: a lasting effect is recast when it expires (or at its cooldown, if later), a one-shot every cooldown. An interval shorter than the duration means rotating that many targets. Untick a power to leave it out of the budget. Generate ops is the only source of ops counted.
  • A power creep lives ticks between renewals at a power spawn or power bank.
  • Data: POWER_INFO from Screeps' common/lib/constants.js; effects as the engine 4.2 processor applies them (each power's source file is in its row's tooltip).
